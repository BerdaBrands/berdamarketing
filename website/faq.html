Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before booking a call.

What makes Berda Marketing different from other agencies?

›

We work on a pay-per-job model instead of charging monthly retainers. You only pay when we actually deliver results, not just for activity.

Do I need to sign a long-term contract?

›

No. There's no lock-in contract. If the results aren't there, you're not stuck paying for a service that isn't working.

What services do you offer?

›

Meta (Facebook and Instagram) lead generation, Google Ads, social media management, and website design, everything a local service business needs to get found and get booked.

What kind of businesses do you work with?

›

We work exclusively with local service businesses, HVAC, cleaning, landscaping, roofing, detailing, auto services, and similar trades.

How fast will I start seeing results?

›

Most clients start seeing leads within the first few weeks of launching a campaign, though exact timelines depend on your industry, budget, and service area.

Do you require a minimum ad spend?

›

Ad spend is discussed and set based on your goals and market during the strategy consultation. We'll walk you through what makes sense for your business.

Will I get reports on how my campaigns are performing?

›

Yes. You'll get transparent monthly reporting with clear metrics, leads generated, conversion rates, and cost per lead, so you always know what's working.

Can you build me a website too?

›

Yes. We design mobile-responsive, high-converting websites built to capture and convert leads, and we can host and maintain it for you going forward.

How do I get started?

›

Book a free 30-minute consultation call. We'll go over your business, your goals, and whether we're the right fit, no pressure, no pitch deck.
